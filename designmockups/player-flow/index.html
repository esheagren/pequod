01 · THE PLAYER

String on top, bar below

The chapter string: two behind (dim), the current one (named, red dot), one ahead. Tap any of them to jump. The small cream dot under 35 means a discussion happened there — a bookmark, not a score.

Bottom bar, left to right: chart, speed · ↺ play ↻ · discuss, settings. Discuss is always there, on every chapter, mid-chapter or not.

02 · SETTINGS

The essay is a preference

“After each chapter: play Claude’s take, then go on.” On, the flow is chapter → take → next chapter with no stop. Off, chapters run straight into each other like any audiobook. Either way the take is still reachable from the discussion button.

“Pause at notes” is the existing behavior from the audio pilot, now opt-in.

03 · CHAPTER END · TAKE ON

The take, then straight on

With the setting on, the chapter ends and the take plays as a track of its own — same transport, same bar. “Up next” shows where you’re headed and when. Nothing asks you anything. Discuss stays where it always is if the take makes you want to argue.

05 · DISCUSSION

Entered on purpose

Pressing discuss opens the conversation for this chapter, over the player, with the string still on top so you never lose your place. The companion opens with a two-sentence version of its take and one question. The full take is a button on the left of the mic if you never heard it.

“Back to the book” returns you to exactly where the narration was.

06 · DISCUSS · MID-CHAPTER

The same button, partway through

Discussion is position-aware. Opened mid-chapter it says where you are, promises not to run ahead of you, and the “I’m lost” question from round one lives here naturally — no separate interrupt gesture needed. The take stays locked until the chapter ends, so it can’t spoil.

07 · TAPPING THE STRING

A chapter is a place you can go back to

Long-press (or tap) a chapter in the string and it unfolds: listen again, the take, resume the discussion you had there, or open the text. The string is navigation and memory at once — it’s how the “club remembers” shows up without a notification.

react toTap-to-jump vs tap-to-unfold? And should the string show the chart (all 138) if you pull down on it?

Changes from round one

The take is no longer a stage in the flow; it’s a preference. The book club is no longer something you fall into after the take; it’s a room you walk into with a button, at any point in any chapter, and the companion knows where you are when you walk in. The hold-to-talk interrupt and its three register chips are gone as separate UI — “I’m lost” is just the first thing you might say after pressing discuss. Still absent: scores, correct answers, restatement menus.
